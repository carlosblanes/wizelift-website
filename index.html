How it works

Your phone goes on the bar in ten seconds.

It rides on the end of the bar, outside the plates.

Your phone stays there for the whole set and you read it between sets. Nothing on your wrist, no cable to the floor, no tripod.

The app

Speed tells you how hard the set was.

As you tire, the bar slows down. Wizelift measures the speed of every rep, so you can see how close you are to your limit.

  1. 01

    Speed, out loud

    You hear each rep's speed as you finish it. Your eyes stay on the bar.

  2. 02

    A limit you choose

    Set a velocity loss of 10, 20, 30 or 40 %. When your reps slow past it, Wizelift tells you to stop.

  3. 03

    Sets that close themselves

    Rack the bar and the set is saved. The rest timer is already running.

  4. 04

    Your next weight

    A suggested load for the next set, worked out from your own lifting.

  5. 05

    1RM without a max test

    Estimated from your normal sets, and tracked session after session.

  6. 06

    Progress, lift by lift

    Every lift marked as rising, holding or slipping. Records and full history.

Your 1RM, estimated from your normal sets.

Heavier bars move slower, in a straight line. Wizelift draws that line from your everyday sets and reads your 1RM off the end of it. The more you train, the better the line.

  • The sensorThe accelerometer and gyroscope already in your phone, read many times a second.
  • The methodMotion sensors drift. Every time the bar stops, Wizelift resets to zero, so the error never builds up.
  • The limitLike every motion sensor, it is least precise on very slow reps near your max.

Frequently asked questions

Which phones does it work with? Is there an Android app?

For now the Wizelift app is iPhone-only, in beta. There is no Android app for buyers yet: if you have an Android phone, join the Android waitlist below and we will tell you when it is ready, before you buy. The clip itself holds any phone: a thin steel ring sticks to the back of your phone or its case, and the phone snaps onto the magnet on the clip.

How do I get the app after I buy?

The app is not in the App Store yet. After your order we email you access to the iPhone beta, to the address you used at checkout.

Which bars does the clip fit?

Any Olympic or powerlifting barbell. It clips onto the 50 mm (2-inch) end where the plates go, not the part you hold. It does not fit the thinner 1-inch bars. A Smith machine, trap bar or hex bar works if its ends are that same 50 mm size.

Which lifts can I use it on?

Lifts where you lower the bar under control: squat, bench press, overhead press, rows, and deadlifts you lower rather than drop. Clip it on the end of the bar, just outside the plates. Do not use it on lifts where the bar is dropped (snatch, clean and jerk, or dropped deadlifts): a dropped bar will throw the phone off.

Is my phone too big, or my case too thick?

The clip is designed for a phone of up to about 280 g including its case, held lengthwise along the bar. The steel ring sticks on the outside of the case, so the magnet holds the ring, not the case: what matters is a flat area on the back about 55 mm wide for the ring to stick to.

Will my phone fall off?

No, on lifts where you lower the bar under control. Silicone strips grip the bar and the clip stays on between sets; a magnet holds the phone to the clip. Like any magnetic mount it is not made for impacts, which is why dropped lifts are out.

When will it arrive?

Each kit is 3D-printed to order in your colour after you pay, and we pack and send it within 24–48 h of payment. Delivery then takes 2–4 working days in mainland Spain and 4–7 days in the rest of the EU; pickup in Madrid is free. Shipping is free over €40. For the Balearic Islands, write to us for a shipping quote. We cannot ship to the Canary Islands, Ceuta or Melilla: they are outside the EU VAT area.

You can also withdraw within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason; the return postage is yours. Shipping, returns & warranty

Is the app free? What is Premium?

Yes. What the kit needs (measuring your sets, rep counting, bar speed and your workout history) needs no subscription, and our terms keep it free for at least 3 years from delivery of your kit. An optional Wizelift Premium with extras such as long-range trends is planned; it is not on sale today and the kit never needs it. Terms of use, sections 13–14

How can a phone be accurate?

It carries the same kind of motion sensors as dedicated VBT devices. Wizelift removes gravity, filters the noise and cancels the error every time the bar stops. Like every motion sensor, it is less precise on very slow reps near your max.
