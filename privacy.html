3.2 Data generated when you use the app

  • Phone sensor data. During a set, the app reads the accelerometer and gyroscope (about 100 readings per second) to calculate bar velocity, power, range of motion and stability for each rep. We store each rep's metrics and the set's motion signal in your account, so you can see each rep's chart later. This is movement data of the bar or phone, not of your body.
  • Helping improve rep counting (only if you turn it on). If you turn on "Help improve rep counting" (off by default; we ask the first time you tell us whether a set was counted right, and it is in Settings → Privacy), we also keep your answers on whether a set was counted right (the real count, quick reasons and an optional note) and the raw motion recording of each set you lift (the accelerometer and gyroscope readings before any processing, about 0.4 MB per set). Only the Wizelift team uses them, only to test and improve rep counting and the metrics. Other users and coaches never see them. Sets a training partner does on your phone are not recorded. Turn it off and nothing more is sent, and the recordings and your answers are deleted within a day.
  • Calculated results. Estimated maxes, load-velocity profiles, fatigue, readiness ("primed / steady / recover") and comparisons (see §9).
  • Your sessions. Our sign-in system (Supabase Auth) records, for each device or browser where you are signed in, when the session started and was last refreshed, the IP address and the browser or app description (user agent) it was opened from. It keeps them while that session is open (see §12).
  • Attempt limits. To stop password guessing and spam, sign-up, sign-in, website forms and shop checkout count attempts per IP. For that we store only an irreversible fingerprint (a keyed hash) of the IP, never the IP itself, and delete it after 90 days.
  • Server logs. Our database provider briefly keeps technical request logs (including IP) to run and protect the service.

5. Health data

5.1 What we treat as health data

We treat as health data (GDPR Art. 9) your body weight and its history, body-fat percentage, resting heart rate, the pain level you note on a set, the "Poor sleep" and "Sore" session tags, and everything we read from Apple Health or Health Connect. We do so because, together, they let someone infer things about your health. Your height and sex are not health data in themselves, but they describe your body, so we keep them under the same consent: without it they are not used in any calculation, comparison or leaderboard, and are not shown to friends or coaches.

5.2 Legal basis: your explicit consent

We only process this data with your explicit consent, given on a separate screen with a box that is not pre-ticked. We record when you gave it and which text you accepted.

  • You can say no. You can still log workouts and use the app. Only the calculations that depend on this data stop working.
  • You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Health data. When you withdraw it, we stop using and receiving this data at once: our server no longer uses your weight, height or sex in any score, leaderboard or comparison (yours or anyone else's), and the app stops reading Apple Health / Health Connect and deletes the readings it kept on the phone. We also offer to delete what we already hold. Withdrawing does not make what we did earlier with your permission unlawful.
  • The system permission (the Apple Health or Health Connect sheet) is a different thing: it is the operating system's permission for the app to read the data. Our consent is the one we ask for, as controller.

5.3 What happens to Apple Health and Health Connect readings

  • Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and the other readings stay on your phone. We use them there to compare today with your own baseline ("less recovered than usual"). We do not send them to our servers or to anyone else.
  • The only reading uploaded to our server is body weight, and only if you turn on "Save my scale weight to Wizelift" (Settings → Privacy, under Health data), which is off by default. It is used to calculate load on bodyweight exercises and to show your trend.
  • We do not use Health / Health Connect data for advertising or data mining, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties. We never send it to any AI service.
  • You can remove the permission at any time in the Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) settings.

5.4 Wizelift is not a medical device

Wizelift is a training and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Recovery and load suggestions are guidance only. If you have pain or an injury, see a healthcare professional.

6.1 Coaches

Anyone with an account can coach, and anyone can have a coach. A coach sees your training only if you let them:

  • How it starts. The coach gives you a code (or its QR or link). You enter it, the app shows you who the coach is and what they will see, you choose, and you accept. A coach cannot ask to follow you; there is no request to say no to. Nothing is shared until you accept.
  • What a coach always sees while the link is active: your finished workouts with their sets, reps, loads, velocities and motion signal, the general workout notes, and your training plan. This applies whatever your workout visibility is (§6), because training is what a coaching link is for. They also see your name, photo and level.
  • What you choose, coach by coach (you can change it at any time): your training profile (goal, experience, height), your sex, and your body weight. Body weight is health data: it is off by default and has its own tick, which is your explicit consent for that coach (§5). Height, sex and body weight reach a coach only while you also have health consent.
  • What a coach never sees: your pain level, perceived exertion, "felt heavy" and the other per-set notes only you see, the "Poor sleep" and "Sore" tags, your Apple Health / Health Connect readings, body fat, heart rate, date of birth or age, and the sets you logged for a guest partner.
  • Record of what you agreed. Each time you accept a coach or change what they see, we record what you chose, when, and the version of the text you were shown. We keep that record so we can show what you agreed to, and you can ask us for a copy.
  • Downloads. A coach can download your sessions as a spreadsheet file (CSV). We record every file a coach downloads from the app (which coach, when, what): our server writes the record before it hands over the data, so a download cannot skip it. A coach can still copy by hand what they can see on screen; that is not recorded. Once downloaded, that file is on the coach's computer and the coach is responsible for it under data-protection law; we cannot delete it. If you want a coach to delete a file, ask them; if they do not, write to us.
  • Ending it. You or the coach can end the link at any time: Settings → Coaching → (the coach) → Remove coach. Access stops at that moment, because every read checks the live link. Blocking each other ends it too. A coach keeps no access to anything after that, except files they had already downloaded.
  • If you are a coach: you must use what you see only to coach that person, keep any file you download secure, and delete it when you stop coaching them. The Terms of Use set out these duties.

If we ever turn on an AI feature, it will be optional. Before it starts we will update this policy (naming the provider and the data sent) and ask for your consent.

9. Automated decisions and profiling

The app automatically calculates things about your training: your estimated max (1RM), your load-velocity profile, your fatigue, whether today you are "primed", "steady" or should "recover", which load to suggest and, if you allow it, your position on leaderboards. This is profiling in the GDPR sense (Art. 4(4)).

  • These are simple, explainable calculations: averages, thresholds and regression lines on your own data. They are not AI systems.
  • We make no decision that has legal effects on you or similarly significantly affects you (GDPR Art. 22). Suggestions are suggestions: you decide what you lift.
  • If you want to know how something is calculated, ask us.

15. Menores

Wizelift es solo para personas de 14 años o más. Lo comprobamos con la fecha de nacimiento al registrarte, y nuestro servidor también rechaza las cuentas de menores de esa edad. En España, a partir de los 14 años un menor puede consentir por sí mismo el tratamiento de sus datos (art. 7 LOPDGDD), incluido el consentimiento explícito para datos de salud de §5. 16. Seguridad

Aplicamos medidas técnicas y organizativas adecuadas al riesgo (art. 32 RGPD). Entre otras:

  • Los datos se guardan en la UE, en discos cifrados (AES-256, según nuestro proveedor) y viajan siempre cifrados (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Cada usuario solo puede acceder a sus propios datos y a lo que otros han decidido compartir con él. Esto se controla en la base de datos, no solo en la app.
  • Las contraseñas se guardan con hash; los tokens de servicios conectados, cifrados con una clave aparte. Los límites de intentos solo guardan una huella irreversible de la IP; la IP en claro solo se guarda con una sesión abierta (§3.2).
  • Las fotos se guardan en almacenamiento privado y se vuelven a codificar al subirlas, lo que elimina su ubicación y otros metadatos.
  • Tu sesión se guarda en el almacén seguro del móvil; en la app web, en el almacenamiento local del navegador, protegido por una política que solo deja ejecutar código de wizelift.com.
  • Nuestras copias de seguridad están cifradas (AES-256) y se guardan en un disco cifrado; conservamos las 30 más recientes. El acceso de administración se limita a quien lo necesita.

Ningún sistema es 100 % seguro. Si sufrimos una brecha de seguridad que afecte a tus datos, la comunicaremos a la AEPD en un máximo de 72 horas cuando la ley lo exija y, si supone un riesgo alto para ti, te avisaremos sin demora (arts. 33 y 34 RGPD).

Si encuentras un fallo de seguridad, escríbenos a support@wizelift.com (ver también security.txt).
