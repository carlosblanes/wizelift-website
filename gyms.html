How members use it

Tap. Train. Climb the board.

Every station carries a tag with NFC and QR. The member taps their phone (or scans the code), the exercise opens with the machine already set, and the set counts toward your gym's leaderboard.

  • NFC and QR on one tag. Works with any modern phone.
  • No wifi, cables or tablets. The tag needs no battery.
  • Sweat-proof industrial adhesive. Removes without marks.

FAQ

Gym questions

Do my members have to pay?

No. The app and leaderboards are free for your members. They can buy their own kit or use the clips you lend or sell at reception.

Do I need wifi or any wiring?

No. NFC tags have no battery and the app runs on each member's phone. Only the leaderboard screens need a TV with a browser.

What about privacy?

No member appears on the leaderboards until they switch it on themselves, and then under the name they chose for their profile. The gym sees aggregated usage only.

Will it damage my machines?

No. Tags use a sweat-proof industrial adhesive and come off without marks.

Do you install outside Madrid?

Yes, across Spain. Outside Madrid a travel fee may apply — it will be in your quote.

Is there a minimum term?

The plan is monthly. We suggest 12 months to make the installation worthwhile, but we'll agree it in your quote.
